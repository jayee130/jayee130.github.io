About Me

Hello! I am Zhijie Jaye Wang, a PhD student in Second Language Acquisition (SLA) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, with minors in Educational Psychology and Chinese Linguistics.

My research examines the dynamic relationship between emotions and engagement in foreign language learning and how this relationship is shaped by instructional and social contexts.

I also investigate task-based language teaching and learning (TBLT), with a particular interest in how TBLT can be effectively integrated into Chinese language pedagogy to foster engagement, communication, and language development.

In addition, I am interested in advancing research methodology in SLA, particularly through longitudinal, multilevel, and dyadic approaches for investigating foreign language learning as a dynamic process.
